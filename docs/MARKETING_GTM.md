## Ideal Customer Profiles (ICP)

### ICP 1: Fashion/Clothing Facebook Sellers (Highest Priority)
- **Who:** Women selling clothes/accessories on Facebook, 1k-50k followers, taking orders in comments
- **Pain:** Manual order tracking in Messenger, no way to show live stock, bKash transfers untracked
- **Why Ab9d wins:** Unified stock (can't oversell), bKash built-in, WhatsApp order alerts, profit tracking
- **Where to find:** F-Commerce Bangladesh, Women Entrepreneurs BD, Mirpur Business Network FB groups
- **Deal size:** Shuroo or Byabsa (৳499–1,499/mo)

### ICP 2: Physical Shop Owners Adding Online Channel
- **Who:** Clothing/electronics/cosmetics shop in Mirpur, Dhanmondi, Bashundhara with walk-in + some Facebook presence
- **Pain:** Selling online and offline with separate stock counts → overselling
- **Why Ab9d wins:** POS + online = same inventory, same order dashboard
- **Where to find:** Cold visit to markets, BGMEA events, BASIS/DCCI networking
- **Deal size:** Byabsa (৳1,499/mo)

### ICP 3: Growing Brands (2–10 employees)
- **Who:** Fashion, cosmetics, or food brand with website or Shopify, >200 orders/month, needs analytics
- **Pain:** Shopify too expensive, no BD payment support, USD pricing
- **Why Ab9d wins:** Local currency, local payments, profit-first analytics, audit trail
- **Where to find:** BD Ecommerce Association, BASIS, LinkedIn
- **Deal size:** Udyog (৳3,999/mo)

## 30-Day Launch Sequence

### Week 1–2: Beta Merchant Recruitment
- Identify 5–10 beta merchants (clothing + F-commerce) personally
- Offer free Byabsa tier for 30 days in exchange for honest feedback + video testimonial
- Join 10 relevant FB groups — participate, answer questions, NO pitching for 2 weeks

### Week 3–4: Soft Launch
- Launch free trial registration page (once manual approval SLA is staffed)
- Start Facebook Lead Ads (৳500 test budget) targeting BD merchant page owners 500-50k followers
- Publish first 3 Facebook posts from content calendar

### Month 2: Scale
- Activate referral program: 1 free month per successful merchant referral
- Launch comparison page (Ab9d vs Shopify/WooCommerce) for SEO
- Approach DCCI or BASIS for SME webinar partnership
- Record YouTube video with a real beta merchant
